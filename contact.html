Frequently Asked Questions

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly request 24 hours notice for any cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice may be subject to a 50% cancellation fee.

Do you offer consultations before a major color change?

Absolutely. We highly recommend a complimentary 15-minute consultation prior to any significant color correction or transformation to ensure we meet your expectations perfectly.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes, all of our house products and the brands we partner with are 100% cruelty-free, sustainable, and formulated with high-quality organic ingredients whenever possible.

Can I book a private event or bridal party?

Yes, we love hosting bridal parties and private events. Please email our concierge at hello@aurasalon.com with your event details to arrange a customized package.
