Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

While we do accept walk-ins based on availability, we highly recommend booking an appointment to ensure our experts are ready for your personalized session.

Can I reschedule?

Absolutely. We kindly ask that you provide at least 24 hours notice for rescheduling to avoid any cancellation fees.

Which stylist should I choose?

If you are unsure, you can select "Any Available" or request a free consultation where we will match you with the perfect expert for your hair or skin type.

What products do you use?

We strictly use premium, cruelty-free, and organic luxury brands that nourish your skin and hair while delivering long-lasting results.

Are bridal consultations free?

Yes, your initial 30-minute bridal consultation is complimentary. We discuss your vision, dress, and trial dates.
