Frequently Asked Questions

How long should I book in advance?

For standard services, we recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance. For bridal and large packages, please book at least 3-6 months prior.

Which facial suits sensitive skin?

Our Classic Facial and Hydra Facial can be completely customized with gentle, hypoallergenic serums perfect for sensitive skin types.

Do you use organic products?

Yes, we prioritize premium, cruelty-free, and organic product lines across our hair coloring, spa, and skincare treatments.

Are bridal trials included?

Bridal trials are included in our "Premium Bridal Journey" package. They can also be booked as a standalone service.

How should I prepare before my appointment?

For color treatments, arrive with unwashed hair (1-2 days). For facials, please avoid retinol products 48 hours prior.
